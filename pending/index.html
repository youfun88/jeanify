What do San Diego sellers ask most?

  1. Do you sell homes in Carmel Valley and 4S Ranch?
    Yes. Along with the coastal corridor, I work the North County master-planned communities: Carmel Valley, Pacific Highlands Ranch, 4S Ranch, Del Sur, Carlsbad and Rancho Santa Fe. My listing-side closings include 17134 Monterey Ridge Way in 4S Ranch.
  2. How long does it take to sell a home in San Diego?
    As of June 2026 the county median was 18 days on market, though that varies enormously by submarket — La Jolla has been running closer to 37 days. Add about four weeks of preparation before listing, and roughly 30 days of escrow after you accept an offer. A realistic end-to-end plan is eight to ten weeks from first conversation to closing.
  3. What are closing costs when I sell in San Diego?
    Plan on roughly 6%–8% of the sale price all in. The largest line is agent compensation, which is negotiable and agreed in writing up front. I give every seller a written net-proceeds estimate before we list, so the number at closing is not a surprise.
  4. Should I make repairs before listing?
    Small ones, yes; large renovations, usually not. Plan on 1–3% of the home's value, weighted toward paint, deep cleaning, landscaping and staging. A pre-listing inspection is worth doing so that surprises become decisions you make on your own timeline.

聖地牙哥賣家最常問什麼？

  1. 您有在 Carmel Valley 和 4S Ranch 賣房嗎？
    有。除了沿海一帶，我也經營北郡的規劃社區：Carmel Valley、Pacific Highlands Ranch、4S Ranch、Del Sur、Carlsbad 與 Rancho Santa Fe。我代表賣方成交的物件，包括 4S Ranch 的 17134 Monterey Ridge Way。
  2. 在聖地牙哥賣一間房子要多久？
    2026 年 6 月全郡的市場停留天數中位數是 18 天，但各區差異很大，La Jolla 大約是 37 天。上市前的準備通常抓四週，接受出價後的交割約 30 天。從第一次洽談到完成交割，比較實際的估計是八到十週。
  3. 在聖地牙哥賣房，過戶費用大概多少？
    整體大約抓售價的 6% 到 8%。其中最大一筆是經紀報酬，可以談，並會事先書面約定。我會在上市前給每位賣方一份書面淨收益試算，讓交割時的數字不會是意外。
  4. 上市前需要先整修嗎？
    小修繕要，大裝修通常不必。建議抓房屋價值的 1% 到 3%，重點放在油漆、深度清潔、庭園整理與佈置。上市前先自己驗一次屋也很值得，這樣意外會變成您在自己的時間表上做的決定。
