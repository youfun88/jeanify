What do San Diego buyers ask most?

  1. Do I have to sign an agreement before you show me homes?
    Yes. Since the 2024 NAR settlement, agents using the MLS must have a written buyer agreement in place before touring a home with you. It is fully negotiable: it states what I do, how long it runs, and exactly how I'm paid. I'm happy to start with a short, single-property or limited-term agreement.
  2. What are closing costs for a buyer in San Diego?
    Typically 2%–5% of the purchase price: lender charges, the lender's title policy and your share of escrow. Budget separately for the supplemental property tax bill that arrives 3–9 months after closing, which your lender does not escrow for.
  3. What is Mello-Roos and will I have to pay it?
    Mello-Roos is a special tax funding infrastructure in newer California developments. In newer master-planned communities such as 4S Ranch, Del Sur and Pacific Highlands Ranch it commonly runs $1,000 to $5,000+ per year, while older Carmel Valley tracts often have none. It is parcel-specific, so check it before you write an offer.
  4. Do you work with first-time buyers?
    Frequently. About a third of my buyer clients are buying their first home. I budget extra time for education, lender introductions, and walking you through every contingency without jargon, in English, Mandarin or Taiwanese.

聖地牙哥買家最常問什麼？

  1. 看房前一定要先簽約嗎？
    是的。自 2024 年產業和解後，使用 MLS 的經紀人在帶看之前，必須先與買方簽署書面委託。內容完全可以談：協議會寫明我提供什麼服務、期間多長、報酬怎麼算。我也很樂意從單一物件或短期的委託開始。
  2. 在聖地牙哥買房，過戶費用大概多少？
    通常是房價的 2% 到 5%，包括貸款相關費用、貸方產權保險，以及您負擔的履約保證費用。另外要單獨預留補充房屋稅：它在交割後 3 到 9 個月才寄來，而且不會由貸款方代收。
  3. 什麼是 Mello-Roos？我需要繳嗎？
    Mello-Roos 是加州較新開發社區用來支應公共建設的特別稅。在 4S Ranch、Del Sur、Pacific Highlands Ranch 這類新規劃社區，每年常見落在 1,000 到 5,000 美元以上；Carmel Valley 較早期的社區則往往完全沒有。它是逐筆地號認定的，下斡旋前務必先查清楚。
  4. 您接首購族嗎？
    經常。我的買方客戶大約三分之一是第一次買房。我會多留一些時間做說明、介紹貸款專員，並用白話帶您走過每一項條件與但書，國語、台語或英語都可以。
